# Art Direction Bible

The visual target is cold 1990s arcade gothic horror: an isolated mansion under a threatening sky, exaggerated architecture, blue-green moonlight, dirty amber interiors and red/green laboratory lighting. Creatures remain grotesque but readable at rail-shooter speed.

## Arcade 2.5D

- 4:3 protected gameplay frame with pillarboxing.
- Moderate internal resolution, crisp sprites, restrained texture filtering.
- High contrast, short impact flashes and dense fog silhouettes.
- UI reproduces the left/right player split without importing the original logo or interface art.

## HD 2.5D

- 16:9 composition with lateral scenery extension, never a wider hit area.
- Higher viewport resolution, smoother particles and lighting.
- Identical timing, hitboxes, enemy count, aim space and scoring.

Procedural architecture is deliberately original while following the documented Curien Mansion route function. Missing licensed geometry is tracked separately.
